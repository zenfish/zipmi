Inventory scope and known gaps

No OEM inventory gap remains for this exact image. Its 81 handler-registration call sites are confined to standard NetFns 0x00, 0x04, 0x06, 0x0a, and 0x0c plus DCMI group 0xdc. The separate zipmi openbmc command is an index of vendor-added provider flavors and must not be applied to this baseline image.
