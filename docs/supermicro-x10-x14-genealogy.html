Risk interpretation

X14 removes weak cipher suites and turns several X10 shell/configuration paths into compatibility stubs, reducing legacy attack surface. It also adds distinct provisioning, D-Bus, storage, file, GPIO, and raw-memory operations. A dropped command can therefore be both a security improvement and a loss of diagnostic or recovery capability.
