Inventory scope and known gaps

All effective addresses are represented. Selector evidence is labeled known, conditional, or unresolved instead of counting control-flow cases as operations. Many field meanings, response layouts, interface-mask names, indirect authorization checks, and completion-code maps remain explicitly partial or unknown. Only four read-only routes have reviewed execution contracts.

Live evidence

Four no-mutation probes were captured on the disposable XD670 zBMC guest. 0x32/0xa6 and 0x32/0x44 succeeded; 0x30/0xe0 returned 0xc7 and 0x32/0x9d returned 0xff. Error completion codes are not reported as success.
